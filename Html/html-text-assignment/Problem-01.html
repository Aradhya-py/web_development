GLA University, Greater Noida Campus

About the College

GLA University expanded its prestigious academic legacy from Mathura into Delhi NCR with its state-of-the-art Greater Noida branch located in Knowledge Park II. This campus stands out as an industry-focused hub, serving as North India's first Google Agentic AI Campus and hosting advanced infrastructure like a specialized code experience center powered by Capgemini.

Courses Offered

The institute provides highly specialized undergraduate and postgraduate professional options designed to meet technical market demands. The key technical and management tracks include:
• B.Tech in Computer Science Engineering (CSE)
• B.Tech in Artificial Intelligence & Machine Learning
• Bachelor of Computer Applications (BCA) in Data Science
• Bachelor of Business Administration (BBA)
• Master of Business Administration (MBA)

Campus Life

Life outside the classroom at GLA Greater Noida is vibrant, combining technical innovation with rich creative expressions. Students actively participate in technology hackathons, competitive coding tournaments, sports leagues, and multi-cultural campus festivals managed through student-led communities.

Why I Like My College

I appreciate this institution because of its deliberate shift away from traditional rote teaching toward direct, career-driven evaluation models. Learning in small interactive batches under seasoned mentors provides immense corporate exposure, laying a robust launching pad for competitive engineering roles with top-tier packages.
