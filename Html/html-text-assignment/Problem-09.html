Institutional Student Terms & Rules

General Rules

Every student must carry their official physical campus identification card visible at all times while on university property. Failure to produce a valid ID upon request by security personnel may lead to temporary restriction from campus labs.

Students must utilize the campus network and technical lab equipment strictly for academic and research-related tasks.
Explanatory Note: Accessing unauthorized networks or running background cryptocurrency mining scripts will result in immediate suspension of server privileges.

Attendance

Students are required to maintain a minimum of 75% attendance in every registered course to qualify for final evaluations. Any student falling below this threshold without an approved medical certificate will be barred from writing exams.

Attendance records are closed and finalized electronically exactly 5 minutes after the scheduled class start time.
Explanatory Note: Late entries are tracked automatically by the biometric turnstiles, and accumulating three late marks will be penalized as one full day absence.

Examination

All student candidates must report to their assigned examination halls at least 20 minutes before the distributed test time. No student will be allowed to cross the entry gates once the examination papers have been distributed to desks.

Possession of unauthorized electronic devices, smartwatches, or loose paper sheets inside the testing workspace constitutes a severe academic infraction.
Explanatory Note: Any candidate caught using unapproved materials will face an automatic zero score on the exam and a mandatory review by the disciplinary committee.

Assignment Submission

All weekly software coding tasks and project documentation files must be uploaded cleanly to the official learning management portal. Code submissions must compile with zero syntax errors to receive evaluation credits from the grading mentors.

The standard cutoff time for lab assignments is firmly set for every Sunday night at 11:59 PM.
Explanatory Note: Submissions sent after this window will face an immediate 20% point deduction per day late, capped at a maximum of 48 hours past deadline.

Discipline

Students must strictly uphold academic integrity standards and maintain professional decorum across all digital and physical university forums. Bullying, harassment, or intentional destruction of campus infrastructure will be met with zero tolerance.

Any verified instance of source code plagiarism or copy-pasting code from peers without explicit attribution carries extreme academic consequences.
Explanatory Note: A first-time plagiarism offense results in immediate suspension from campus placement drives for a duration of one full semester.
