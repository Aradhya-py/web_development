GLA University

GLA University is a university located in Mathura, Uttar Pradesh. It offers undergraduate and postgraduate programs in areas such as engineering, management, computer applications and other disciplines.

About GLA University

GLA University focuses on providing students with academic knowledge along with practical learning opportunities. The university encourages students to develop technical skills, problem-solving abilities and professional knowledge.

The university provides an environment where students can participate in academics, projects, events and other activities during their studies.

Programs

GLA University offers programs in different fields. Engineering students can choose from areas such as Computer Science and Engineering, Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning, Mechanical Engineering and Electronics.

Students can also explore programs in management, pharmacy, education and other disciplines according to their interests.

Technology-focused programs provide students with opportunities to develop practical and industry-relevant skills.

Campus Life

Campus life at GLA University includes academic activities, technical events, cultural programs, sports and student organisations. These activities give students opportunities to learn outside the classroom and work with other students.

Students can take part in projects, competitions, workshops and college events to gain practical experience and improve their communication and teamwork skills.

Admission

Students interested in joining GLA University can check the admission requirements, available programs and application information through the official admission portal.

Admissions are subject to the eligibility requirements of the selected program.
