Agra Travel Guide

Agra is a famous city in India known for its historical monuments, Mughal architecture, rich culture and delicious local food. It is a popular destination for a weekend trip.

1. Attractions

Taj Mahal

The Taj Mahal is a magnificent white marble monument located in Agra, India. It was commissioned by Mughal Emperor Shah Jahan in memory of his wife Mumtaz Mahal. Built between 1631 and 1648, it is admired for its beautiful architecture, symmetry and detailed marble work.

Agra Fort

Agra Fort is another important historical attraction in the city. The massive red sandstone fort contains beautiful palaces, halls and other structures associated with the Mughal period.

Learn more about the Taj Mahal at UNESCO .

2. Culture

Agra has a strong Mughal cultural influence. Visitors can explore historical buildings, traditional markets and local handicrafts. The city's architecture reflects its rich history and cultural heritage.

3. Food

Agra offers a variety of delicious local food influenced by its Mughlai heritage. Visitors should try the famous Agra Petha, a traditional sweet made from ash gourd, along with Dalmoth, kachori, chaat and other local snacks. The city's markets are also great places to experience traditional Agra flavours.

4. Best Time to Visit

The best time to visit Agra is generally from October to March, when the weather is more comfortable for sightseeing. The cooler months are suitable for exploring the Taj Mahal, Agra Fort and other attractions. Winter is a popular season for tourism.

5. Travel Tips

Important Travel Tip: Start your sightseeing early to make the most of your day and avoid the busiest periods. Wear comfortable footwear, carry drinking water and keep your belongings secure.

Plan your attractions in advance and keep some extra time for local traffic when travelling between different places in Agra.

6. Plan Your Visit

Before visiting Agra, check official websites for information about attractions, timings and visitor guidelines. These resources can help you plan your trip and learn more about the city's important historical sites.
