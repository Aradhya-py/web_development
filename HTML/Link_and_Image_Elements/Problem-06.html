TechFest 2026

Where innovation meets creativity and technology!

Introduction

TechFest 2026 is a college technology festival designed for students who are passionate about computers, programming, artificial intelligence and innovation. The event provides an opportunity to learn, compete and share ideas with other technology enthusiasts.

Highlights

Participants can experience coding competitions, AI challenges, project demonstrations and technology discussions. The festival will encourage students to develop their problem-solving skills and turn creative ideas into practical projects.

Major Highlight: A special AI innovation challenge will allow students to present their ideas and compete with other teams.

Date and Venue

Date: 20 September 2026
Time: 10:00 AM onwards
Venue: GLA University, Noida

Speakers

The event will feature technology professionals, educators and experienced developers who will discuss modern technologies, artificial intelligence and the future of computer science.

Guest speakers will share their experiences and advice with students interested in building careers in technology.

Registration

Registration closes on 15 September 2026. Students are encouraged to register before the deadline to reserve their place at the event.
